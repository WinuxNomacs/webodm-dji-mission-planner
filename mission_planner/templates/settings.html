Lidar surface of the 3D view

Where USGS lidar covers a route (most of the US), the editors' 3D view shows a 1 m surface with trees and buildings, and the route is checked against it for collisions. It is built from the public lidar when a route is opened and kept here for every route nearby (about 12 MB and 100 MB of download a km²). Clearing removes it; it is built again when a route there is opened.

Import routes from LuxODM

Brings over the routes of LuxODM's mission planner, from the file LuxODM writes with python manage.py export_routes <file>. Each route goes to the account with its LuxODM owner's username and gets a new id (bookmarked LuxODM editor addresses do not carry over); a route whose owner has no account here is skipped. Importing a file again imports its routes again, as copies.
